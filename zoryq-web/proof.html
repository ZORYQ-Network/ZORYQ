USE IT YOURSELF

Agent-to-Agent Economy Lab

Use testnet ZQ only. For the full two-agent flow, use two MetaMask/Rabby accounts. Account A creates the job; Account B bids and submits; Account A assigns and settles. Every button below creates a real ZORYQ testnet transaction and your wallet must approve it.

1 · Register current wallet

Creates a Proof-of-Agent identity for the connected account.

2 · Account A creates escrow job

Locks the amount shown in “Escrow ZQ” inside the protocol.

3 · Switch to Account B and bid

Account B must be registered first. Bid must be ≤ escrow cap.

4 · Switch to Account A and assign

Paste Account B into the Worker field, then accept its bid.

5 · Switch to Account B and submit

Writes a deterministic test delivery hash to the task.

6 · Switch to Account A and settle

Releases the accepted payment, refunds unused escrow and increases worker reputation.

Scope

This is a testnet-only, experimental and unaudited prototype. “Agents” here are independent EVM identities with programmable onchain authority and economic state. Autonomous reasoning/models run offchain and must still obey the onchain policies and settlement rules. This is not a claim of mainnet readiness, decentralization, investment return or monetary value for testnet ZQ.
