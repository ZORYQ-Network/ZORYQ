What makes this different?

ZORYQ is experimenting with an agent-native path where actions can be machine-readable, previewed, simulated and verified while wallet signing stays explicit. Pair that with Project Registry identity and verifiable builder activity and you can build products that care not just about transactions, but about who built what and what actually happened on-chain.
