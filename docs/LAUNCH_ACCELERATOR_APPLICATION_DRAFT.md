## Company

ZORYQ Network

## One-liner

ZORYQ is building verifiable economic infrastructure for autonomous AI agents: bounded authority, service hiring, onchain payments, evidence-backed accounting and portable Proof Packs.

## What problem are you solving?

AI agents can increasingly plan and execute multi-step work, but businesses and users still lack a trustworthy way to answer basic economic questions: who authorized the agent, what was it allowed to do, what did it actually execute, what did it pay or receive, and what evidence proves the result?

Today's alternatives fragment identity, permissions, payment and audit evidence across separate systems or depend on the agent/application to self-report what happened.

## Product

ZORYQ's flagship direction is Autonomous Company: a user supplies a goal, bounded budget, duration and autonomy policy; the system creates an inspectable organization of agents/services that can plan work, create tasks, hire services, perform authorized actions, make/receive testnet payments and export a portable evidence pack.

The target lifecycle is:

`goal -> plan -> agents -> tasks/hire -> authorize -> execute -> pay/receive -> verify -> account -> Proof Pack`

## Why now?

The market is moving from AI assistants toward agents that take actions across systems. As those agents become economically active, trust shifts from "what did the model say?" to "what was it allowed to do, what happened, and can another party verify it?"

At the same time, standard EVM tooling makes programmable economic settlement increasingly accessible. ZORYQ is focused on the trust/accountability layer between autonomous software and economic execution.

Initial customers are expected to be AI-agent startups, developer platforms, marketplaces or businesses that need auditable delegated automation and verifiable economic actions.

## Competition

Alternatives include generic chains, smart-account/session-key providers, agent frameworks, payment APIs and workflow automation systems.

Our intended differentiation is one coherent model for agent identity, bounded authority, service/task relationships, verifiable execution, payment receipts, accounting and portable Proof Packs.
