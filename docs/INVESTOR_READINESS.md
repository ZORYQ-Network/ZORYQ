# ZORYQ Investor Readiness

ZORYQ is an early-stage experimental public EVM testnet and developer platform. This document explains what is investable today, what is not yet proven, the product wedge, possible revenue models, and the evidence required before a serious fundraising push.

## One-sentence thesis

**ZORYQ is building an evidence-first blockchain and agent-commerce layer where autonomous software can receive bounded authority, hire services, transact, and produce verifiable onchain proof of what it actually did.**

The flagship product direction is **ZORYQ Autonomous Company**:

> Give ZORYQ a goal and a budget. It creates a bounded autonomous company of agents that works, hires, pays, earns and accounts onchain.

See [`ZORYQ_AUTONOMOUS_COMPANY.md`](ZORYQ_AUTONOMOUS_COMPANY.md).

## What exists today

Evidence should be preferred over broad claims.

- Public experimental EVM-compatible testnet.
- Chain ID `5919065`.
- Public RPC and faucet path.
- A reproducible external first-transaction proof runner.
- First independently reported external developer reproduction completed on 2026-09-12; see [`EXTERNAL_TRACTION_EVIDENCE.md`](EXTERNAL_TRACTION_EVIDENCE.md) and Issue #88.
- Developer, network, security, consensus, recovery and autonomous-company work is tracked through public code, issues and evidence gates.
- Autonomous Company is a product thesis and implementation target; capability claims remain gated until public end-to-end evidence exists.

## What must not be claimed yet

ZORYQ should not currently claim:

- production mainnet readiness;
- decentralization;
- audited security;
- proven product-market fit;
- meaningful retained-user scale;
- revenue unless externally collected and documented;
- TVL unless independently measurable;
- token value or investment return;
- that Autonomous Company fully works, hires, pays, earns and accounts onchain until each verb passes its evidence gate.

## Why an investor could care

The intended wedge is not "another generic EVM chain." The investable question is whether ZORYQ can become infrastructure for **economically active AI agents and autonomous organizations** with transparent authority and proof.

The product should make five things inspectable:

1. **Identity** — who or what the agent/company is.
2. **Authority** — exactly what it is allowed to do.
3. **Intent** — what outcome it attempted.
4. **Economic action** — what it hired, paid, received or executed.
5. **Evidence** — receipts, state, accounting and a portable Proof Pack.

If ZORYQ can make this experience simple, verifiable and developer-friendly, the differentiation can come from the product/protocol layer rather than from unproven throughput claims.

## Revenue model hypotheses

These are business-model hypotheses, not current revenue claims.

### 1. Autonomous Company platform fee

A fee can be charged on paid services or budgets executed through a production-safe Autonomous Company platform.

Possible pricing structure:
- free/testnet developer tier;
- fixed monthly business tier;
- usage fee on verified agent-service transactions;
- enterprise/custom policy, compliance and support tier.

### 2. Developer infrastructure

Potential paid services:
- higher-rate RPC/API access;
- indexed event and receipt APIs;
- agent identity/reputation APIs;
- managed Proof Pack/evidence infrastructure;
- production monitoring and observability;
- managed deployment and support.

### 3. Agent/service marketplace

Where legally and technically appropriate, ZORYQ can charge a marketplace/service fee when a company discovers and hires an external agent or service.

### 4. Enterprise infrastructure

Potential B2B revenue:
- private/permissioned deployments;
- policy engines;
- evidence/audit tooling;
- enterprise integrations;
- SLA-backed infrastructure and support.

### 5. Protocol/network fees

Future network fees may contribute to protocol economics after a production architecture, security model, legal analysis and token/network design are mature.

**A public token sale is not treated here as the primary revenue plan.** Any token distribution, sale or investment product requires separate legal, economic and security analysis.

| --- | --- | --- |

## The next 30-day objective

Do not optimize for vanity metrics. Optimize for evidence that changes the investment conversation.

Priority sequence:

1. Turn the first independent developer proof into a durable public traction record.
2. Recruit and verify 4-9 additional independent developers.
3. Obtain one truly independently controlled Node 2.
4. Finish one narrow, investor-grade Autonomous Company vertical slice.
5. Get one external person to reproduce that Autonomous flow.
6. Convert one design partner into a paid or formally committed pilot.
7. Only then begin a focused pre-seed investor process with a clean evidence-backed data room.

## What to show in an investor demo

The best demo should take less than five minutes:

1. Create an Autonomous Company with a concrete goal and bounded testnet budget.
2. Show its agent organization and scoped permissions.
3. Show a task/hire relationship.
4. Execute an authorized testnet action/payment.
5. Show the successful chain receipt.
6. Show accounting updating from chain evidence.
7. Export the Proof Pack.
8. Show independent external traction evidence separately.

The demo must visibly distinguish live evidence from roadmap or simulated data.

## Fundraising narrative

Avoid: "We built a blockchain and need money to grow."

Prefer an evidence-backed narrative:

> ZORYQ is building verifiable economic infrastructure for autonomous software. An independent developer has already reproduced our public transaction path from a local machine using only public endpoints. Our next milestone is a reproducible Autonomous Company that can operate under bounded permissions, transact onchain and produce portable proof. We are using external reproduction, independent operators and paid pilots as fundraising gates rather than relying on unverified claims.

## Capital strategy

Preferred order:

1. non-dilutive grants, credits, hackathons and builder programs;
2. paid design partners/pilots;
3. angels with infrastructure/AI-agent expertise;
4. focused pre-seed round after stronger external usage evidence;
5. institutional round only after measurable adoption and a credible security/operations story.

## Metrics to track publicly and privately

Use exact counts, not inflated estimates:

- independent developers who completed first transaction;
- independent Node operators;
- weekly active external wallets excluding project-controlled wallets;
- externally deployed applications/contracts;
- repeat external users;
- Autonomous Companies created by external users;
- verified autonomous tasks/hire/payment/revenue/accounting events;
- paying pilots/customers;
- monthly recurring revenue when it exists;
- open security blockers;
- uptime and failed onboarding attempts.

## Immediate blocker policy

Investor outreach should not outrun product credibility. Red deployment paths, unresolved reproducibility failures, unsafe key handling, misleading claims or broken onboarding should be fixed before broad promotion.

The strongest investor asset ZORYQ can create is a chain of evidence showing that independent people can discover, run, build, operate and eventually pay for the system without privileged assistance.
