## What are you building?

ZORYQ is building verifiable economic infrastructure for autonomous AI agents. The core product direction, Autonomous Company, lets a user give software agents a goal and bounded budget, then makes their identities, permissions, task/service relationships, onchain payments, receipts and accounting inspectable through portable evidence.

The wedge is an Agent Commerce & Trust Layer: who an agent is, what it is allowed to do, what it intended, what it actually executed, what it paid or received, and what evidence supports the result.

## Why does this need crypto / blockchain?

Autonomous software increasingly acts across services and counterparties that do not share one database or trust boundary. We use an EVM-compatible public execution layer to anchor authorization-relevant economic actions, payments and receipts so independent parties can verify outcomes without relying on the agent's own narrative or a single application database.

Blockchain is not used as a cosmetic data store. The product is designed around independently verifiable transaction receipts, identities/references, bounded economic authority and portable Proof Packs.

## What is the most important thing you have learned so far?

Reproducibility is more valuable than feature count at this stage. A single independent developer successfully reproducing the public path changed the credibility of the project more than another internal demo would have. We are now prioritizing independent operators, external apps, repeat developers and design partners over vanity activity.

## Competitive landscape

Alternatives span:
- generic EVM chains;
- smart-account/session-key infrastructure;
- agent frameworks;
- payment APIs;
- workflow automation;
- agent marketplaces.

ZORYQ's intended differentiation is the combination of bounded agent authority, service/task relationships, verifiable economic execution, evidence-backed accounting and portable Proof Packs in one product/protocol layer.

We want pressure-testing from crypto-native founders and investors before making broader production claims.
