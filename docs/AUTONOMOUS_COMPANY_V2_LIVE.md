## What this proves — and what it does not

This proves the **onchain economic-control layer**: company state, treasury, roles, limits, work evidence, verification, payments, revenue accounting, reputation and emergency control are executable and inspectable on the public testnet.

It does **not** prove autonomous AI reasoning or real commercial revenue. The first market opportunity and 40 dUSD revenue are deterministic testnet mechanics. dUSD is synthetic testnet accounting with no monetary value. A real offchain AI orchestrator, real service discovery, real external work verification and production stablecoin settlement remain separate milestones before this can be described as a real autonomous commercial economy.
