# ZORIQ Pricing Experiments

Pricing is tested, not guessed.

## Route fee
Initial hypothesis: **15 bps (0.15%)** for eligible transactions.

Test against:
- route completion rate;
- competitor/provider alternatives;
- fee revenue per active funded wallet;
- support complaints/refunds;
- impact on 30-day retention.

Never silently increase fees. Fee amount and policy version must be visible before authorization.

## ZORIQ Pro
Initial Founders hypothesis: **US$4.99/month**, localized through the platform store.

Test:
- free-to-paid conversion;
- 30/90-day paid retention;
- feature usage;
- support burden;
- willingness to pay by region.

## B2B
Start with partner pilots before fixed public pricing. Meter Guard/ONE usage from day one so unit economics can be measured.

## Guardrail
No pricing experiment may alter transaction output after the user sees the review screen or make a token/airdrop promise.
