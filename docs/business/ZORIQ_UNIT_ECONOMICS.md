# ZORIQ Unit Economics

Revenue projections are scenarios, not promises.

## Route fee formula
`gross_route_revenue = eligible_volume × fee_bps / 10,000`

At 15 bps:
- $1,000 eligible volume -> $1.50 gross fee;
- $100,000 -> $150;
- $1,000,000 -> $1,500;
- $10,000,000 -> $15,000.

Net revenue must subtract partner/rebate costs, infrastructure, fraud/support, taxes and any other contractual costs.

## Pro formula
`gross_subscription_mrr = paid_active_subscribers × localized_store_price`

Store commissions/taxes/refunds reduce net receipts. Use actual platform statements for accounting.

## Goal
Optimize retained funded users and trustworthy successful activity, not fee percentage alone. A lower fee with more retained volume can produce better long-term economics.
