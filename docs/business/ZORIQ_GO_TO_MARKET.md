# ZORIQ Go-to-Market — Revenue First, Trust First

## Positioning
**ZORIQ — Crypto without complexity.**

The wedge is not another token. It is a safer, simpler multichain experience combining wallet, routing, intent, identity and social distribution.

## Launch funnel
1. Install ZORIQ.
2. Create/import/connect a wallet securely.
3. See assets across supported networks.
4. Run Guard on an address/transaction.
5. Complete a swap through ZORIQ Route.
6. Earn useful-activity XP.
7. Create a ZORIQ ID / invite another user.
8. Convert power users to ZORIQ Pro.

## First revenue loops
### Transaction loop
Better route UX -> successful swaps -> eligible ZORIQ route fee -> treasury -> reconciliation -> reinvest in acquisition/security.

### Subscription loop
Free Guard/portfolio value -> clear premium pain point -> ZORIQ Pro -> recurring revenue -> better retention.

### Distribution loop
Social/quests/referrals -> new funded users -> more useful activity -> more route opportunities without rewarding wash trading.

### B2B loop
Proven Guard/ONE inside ZORIQ -> external SDK/API -> developer customers -> usage-based/recurring B2B revenue.

## Founder launch offer hypothesis
Use a limited **ZORIQ Pro Founders** offer around US$4.99/month localized by store. This is a pricing experiment, not a guaranteed final price. Measure conversion, retention and support load before increasing scope.

## What not to do
- do not launch a token just to fund the app;
- do not promise airdrop value;
- do not force users onto a future ZORIQ Network;
- do not hide fees in exchange rates;
- do not pay XP for easy wash volume;
- do not expand mainnet support faster than Guard/security coverage.

## Proof of product-market fit
Prioritize retained funded users, successful routed volume, Guard usage and paid conversion over vanity downloads.
